Carbide Willson Ruins Photogrammetry

As part of a project I lead to digitally document the Carbide Willson Ruins, a significant heritage site in my hometown of Chelsea, QC, I utilized photogrammetry to produce a detailed 3D model of the site. The work included creating a high-resolution model with over 3 million vertices to ensure maximum information preservation, alongside a lower-resolution version optimized for practical applications like visualization and interactive use. This initiative contributed to preserving and sharing the history of this iconic landmark.
