3D Printed Ukuleles

As a personal challenge to test my creative and technical 3D skills, I designed, 3D-printed, painted, and assembled two full-sized ukuleles. While I referred to online guides for some technical reference, the designs are entirely original. The first ukulele, painted to resemble wood, had some minor flaws, including slightly off-tune upper frets, higher-than-ideal action, and a heavy neck. I used this iteration to experiment with different paints, varnishes, assembly glues, and peg mounting techniques. Learning from this process, I redesigned and built a second ukulele that met my expectations. To finish, I painted an ocean sunset scene on the body, inspired by Hawaii-based artist Walfrido Garcia. The final result was easier to play, had a fuller, more in-tune sound, and was more visually interesting.
